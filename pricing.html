Common Questions

PRICING FAQ

  • Is there a joining or registration fee?+

    No joining fees whatsoever. At Cocoon, the price you see is the price you pay — your monthly fee covers everything. First-time members also get their first class completely free with no strings attached.

  • What payment methods do you accept?+

    We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), all major debit/credit cards, net banking, and cash. Monthly fee collection happens on or before the 1st of each month. We'll send a WhatsApp reminder before your due date.

  • Can I pause my membership?+

    Yes! Life happens — illness, travel, exams. You can pause your membership for up to 30 days per year at no additional cost. Simply inform us 3 days in advance and we'll freeze your account from the requested date.

  • What exactly is included in the Unlimited plan?+

    The Combo plan gives you unlimited access to all Zumba classes, including sub-programs (Kids, Gold, Foundation through Advanced). You also get priority booking for workshops and grading exams, plus a welcome merchandise kit for new joiners. It's our best-value plan by far.

  • What is your refund policy?+

    Monthly plans: We offer a prorated refund if you cancel within the first 7 days of a billing cycle. After 7 days, the month's fee is non-refundable. Quarterly and annual plans: 50% refund if cancelled within the first 30 days; no refunds after that. All refunds are processed within 7 business days.

  • Are there student or senior discounts?+

    Yes! Students with a valid ID get 10% off any plan. Seniors (60+) enrolled in Zumba Gold get 15% off the Zumba plan. These discounts cannot be combined but apply on top of quarterly/annual savings. Speak to our front desk to avail.

  • Do you offer family discounts?+

    Absolutely. Families enrolling 2 or more members get 15% off the total combined fee. 3 or more members get 20% off. This applies per billing cycle and is one of our most popular options for families who train together.

  • How does the free trial class work?+

    Every new student gets one completely free trial class — no card required, no commitment. Simply call us, message us on WhatsApp, or fill out the contact form. We'll slot you into the batch that suits you best. Come in, experience the energy, and decide from there.
