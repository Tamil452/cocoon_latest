ZUMBA®
FITNESS

Move Better. Feel Better. Live Better.

Zumba Fitness combines aerobic, interval, and resistance-training principles with energetic music and easy-to-follow dance movements. This scientifically designed format helps improve cardiovascular health, increase calorie expenditure, and enhance overall fitness while making exercise enjoyable and sustainable.

Key Benefits

  • ✔️ Burns calories and supports healthy weight management
  • ✔️ Improves cardiovascular fitness and endurance
  • ✔️ Enhances coordination, balance, and agility
  • ✔️ Strengthens and tones the entire body
  • ✔️ Reduces stress and improves mental well-being
  • ✔️ Increases energy levels and self-confidence

Science-Based Approach

Zumba utilizes aerobic conditioning, interval training, and functional movement patterns that support heart health, muscular endurance, and metabolic fitness.

Community & Well-being

Group participation encourages motivation, social connection, accountability, and a sense of belonging, making fitness an enjoyable shared experience.

✨

THE SECRET TO SUCCESS

The more you participate, the better you feel! Consistency is the key to achieving fitness goals. Because Zumba is fun, energetic, and easy to follow, it helps participants stay committed to an active and healthy lifestyle.

ZUMBA® GOLD
ACTIVE SENIORS

Active Aging for a Healthier Life

Zumba Gold is specially designed for older adults and beginners seeking a safe, effective, and enjoyable way to stay physically active. The program modifies traditional Zumba movements to suit varying fitness levels while maintaining all the fun and energy.

Key Benefits

  • ✔️ Improves mobility and functional fitness
  • ✔️ Enhances balance and stability
  • ✔️ Supports cardiovascular health
  • ✔️ Increases flexibility and range of motion
  • ✔️ Promotes independence and quality of life
  • ✔️ Boosts mood and emotional well-being

Science-Based Approach

Regular movement helps maintain muscle strength, balance, bone health, cognitive function, and overall physical independence as we age.

Community & Well-being

Social engagement is one of the strongest predictors of healthy aging. Zumba Gold provides opportunities to connect, stay active, and build meaningful relationships within the community.

AQUA ZUMBA®
WATER FITNESS

Low Impact on Joints. High Impact on Results.

Key Benefits

  • ✔️ Burns calories effectively
  • ✔️ Improves cardiovascular fitness and stamina

Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED

  • Do I need prior dancing experience to join? +

    Absolutely not! Our Zumba programs are designed with simple, repetitive steps that anyone can follow regardless of dance background. Our instructors are trained to guide you so you feel comfortable from day one. Your first class is free, so come try it!

  • What age groups do you cater to? +

    We have specialized programs for all age groups: Zumba® Kids Jr. (ages 4–6), Zumba® Kids (ages 7–11), Zumba® Gold for active seniors and beginners, Aqua Zumba® for water fitness lovers, and regular Zumba® Fitness for teens and adults. We believe dance fitness is for everyone.

  • What should I wear to a Zumba class? +

    We recommend comfortable athletic wear (such as long-sleeve activewear tops and leggings) and supportive cross-training or fitness shoes that allow easy pivot movements. Please bring a water bottle and a small sweat towel.

  • How many calories can I expect to burn? +

    A typical 60-minute high-energy Zumba class burns between 400 to 600 calories depending on intensity. It's a full-body cardiovascular workout that boosts endurance and tones muscles.
