About the Academy

OUR STORY

A decade of transformation in Coimbatore

Est. 2015

WHERE LEARNING MEETS Growth

Cocoon Academy was born in 2015 with a mission: to create nurturing spaces for learning, physical fitness, and holistic growth. We began as a community fitness hub in Coimbatore, and over a decade of dedication, we have evolved into a multi-branch academy offering premier preschool education, daycare, creative skill development programs, and certified Zumba® fitness classes.

Today, we are home to 800+ active students, certified early educators, and professional movement coaches across our Coimbatore HQ and Kuthalam branch. By integrating academic preschool learning with movement-based wellness, we provide a complete developmental pathway for children, teens, and adults.

Milestones

A DECADE OF Milestones

From a small studio to a state-recognised training academy — here's the journey.

  • 2015
    THE THE COCOON IS BORN

    Founded by Lakshmi Priya with 12 founding students in a 600 sq.ft studio in RS Puram, Coimbatore. The dream of bringing Latin-inspired dance cardio to Coimbatore takes its first breath.

  • 2015
    FIRST CHARITY FITNESS FESTIVAL

    We organize Coimbatore's first major charity Zumba fitness marathon, drawing 200+ dancers. The city takes notice of the Cocoon vibe.

  • 2016
    ZUMBA KIDS PROGRAM LAUNCHES

    Recognising the power of early movement education, we launch Zumba Kids — bringing rhythm and fitness to children aged 5–14. Enrolment doubles within three months.

  • 2018
    CORPORATE WELLNESS EXPANSION

    Cocoon launches its corporate wellness program, delivering Zumba classes to major tech hubs in Coimbatore to reduce workspace fatigue and boost energy.

  • 2020
    RESILIENCE — ONLINE PIVOT

    During the pandemic, Cocoon pivots to live-streamed online classes within a week. We retain 85% of our student base and reach learners in Bangalore, Chennai, and Hyderabad.

  • 2022
    EXPANDED CAMPUS & 500 STUDENTS

    We move to our current 3,000 sq.ft. facility on Arunachalam Street, featuring dedicated double dance studios with shock-absorbent flooring, and cross the milestone of 500 enrolled students.

  • 2024
    10 YEARS · MASSIVE CONCERT

    Our golden decade. We celebrate 10 years of pure joy by hosting a massive outdoor Zumba concert at Gandhipuram, bringing together 600+ students. The cocoon has fully transformed.

  • 2026
    KUTHALAM BRANCH & LEARNING CENTER

    Cocoon Academy expands to a multi-branch network, launching the Kuthalam Branch on Nadu Chetty Street and introducing 'The Cocoon Learning Center' to deliver world-class Kids Learning & Dev programs.

What We Stand For

OUR Core Values

Every class, every session, every milestone is guided by three principles we refuse to compromise on.

💃

VITALITY

Zumba is a celebration of life through movement. We teach with high energy, ensuring every class is a dance fitness party that releases stress and boosts mental wellness. We honor the Latin and global rhythms that give birth to our choreography.

🏆

EXCELLENCE

We never teach to the average — we teach to the individual's full potential. Every student at Cocoon receives personalised attention, structured progression, and access to the same methods that produced 25+ national award-winning champions.

🫂

COMMUNITY

We believe the best transformation happens in community. Cocoon isn't a gym — it's a family. Our students celebrate together, compete together, and lift each other through every challenge. When you join Cocoon, you belong.
